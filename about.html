About

I worked for twenty years with artists and the institutions that hold them: university galleries in New York and Virginia, art schools in Singapore, the United States and Australia, most recently as Head of Studies at the National Art School in Sydney.

In 2016, after Prince Rogers Nelson died, I began a museum that starts from the other side: the people whose lives an artist's work has shaped. The People's Museum for Prince has had two seasons in Minneapolis, in 2018 and 2026, the second with seventy-five contributors from nine countries, and now lives year-round as the Living Archive. It was the practice component of my PhD and it remains the center of my work.

I trained in film at the Victorian College of the Arts and made short films in the 1990s. Dearly Beloved is my return to filmmaking.
