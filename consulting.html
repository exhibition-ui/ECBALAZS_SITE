Consulting

I curate projects about how art and artists transform people's lives. Where an artist's museum usually looks at the artist, I turn the lens to the audience and gather their testimony: art, story and memory. What emerges is a new kind of portrait of the artist, refracted through the people whose lives the work has shaped.

The People's Museum for Prince is the working model: ten years, two Minneapolis seasons, contributors from nine countries, a year-round Living Archive. I bring the same method to artists' estates, artists' museums and institutions, as guest curator, collaborator or adviser. A season, a commission, a residency, a conversation.

I also work with art schools and arts institutions on the question that shaped my academic career: how we prepare artists for the lives they will actually lead. Curriculum, learning and teaching, academic leadership, and the institutional habits that get in the way.
