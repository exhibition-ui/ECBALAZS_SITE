Dearly Beloved, 2026

A meditation on grief, love and transformation after the death of Prince Rogers Nelson. Following the homes where Prince lived, from his birth to his death, the filmmaker searches for traces of the artist in the places he left behind, drawing on nine years of documentation in Minnesota. Documentary, six minutes. Writer and director.
